# Sourcing, facts & media rights

These features are public, name someone real, and are meant to rank — so accuracy and clean media
handling protect both DGTL and the person featured. Treat this as the contract for the whole skill.

## Fact discipline

- **Only publish what you can verify.** Every concrete claim (a role, a metric, a client, an award, a
  date) needs a source you actually saw — the creator's own channel, the brand's site, or reputable press.
- **Never invent** stats, quotes, testimonials, clients, awards, or the claim that someone is "in the DGTL
  Influence network." If the user asserts a fact, you may use it and attribute it to them; otherwise verify
  or omit.
- **Unverifiable → omit, don't guess.** The template's stat slots and gallery tiles are optional. An empty
  slot (it keeps a clean gradient) is always better than a made-up number.
- **Metrics get an as-of date.** Follower/subscriber/view counts move. Phrase as "6M+ subscribers (as of
  <month year>)" or keep it round and current. Prefer the platform itself or a reputable tracker.
- **Keep a Sources list** as you go: `- <fact> — <URL>`. Deliver it with the page and add a brief on-page
  attribution where a claim leans on one source.

## Quotes & copyrighted text

- Quote a **real, named source** and keep it short (roughly a sentence). Attribute it (person, role, and
  where it's from). Never attribute a line to someone who didn't say it.
- **Summarise, don't reproduce.** When you pull from a news article, write the facts in your own words. Do
  not paste paragraphs of someone else's article. At most one short quoted sentence, in quotation marks,
  with attribution and a link.
- Song lyrics: don't reproduce them.

## Screenshots & third-party media

Screenshots of a creator's posts and their press coverage are used here as **editorial reference** — to
show the work the article is about. Handle them cleanly:

- **Attribute every captured image.** The caption/alt should credit the creator (handle) and the source,
  and where possible the page links to the original post/article.
- **Public pages only.** Do **not** log into an account, bypass a paywall, or defeat a CAPTCHA or age-gate
  to capture media. If something is private or gated, ask the user to supply the asset instead.
- **Respect the platform.** Follow robots/ToS; don't hammer a site; capture what you need and move on.
- **Crop to the content.** Avoid grabbing unrelated feed items, other people's comments, ad units, or any
  bystander's personal information.
- **Prefer the cleanest source:** user-supplied media → official embed/oEmbed thumbnail → screenshot of the
  public page. Official embeds carry the platform's own attribution and are the safest.
- **Rights stay with the owner.** These screenshots illustrate an editorial feature; they don't transfer
  ownership. Flag to the user that final publication rights for any embedded media are theirs to confirm,
  especially for commercial use or if a brand's footage is involved.

## People & privacy

- Feature the person in their **public, professional** capacity — their work, platforms, and public
  statements. Don't publish private contact details, home location beyond a city, family information, or
  anything not already public and relevant.
- If the subject is or may be a minor, keep everything strictly professional and age-appropriate, and check
  with the user before publishing.
- If you find conflicting or unflattering unverified claims, leave them out — this is a feature that
  platforms the creator, not an exposé.

## When in doubt

Ask the user for the missing fact, the asset, or a link. A shorter, fully-true page is the goal; there is
never a reason to fabricate to fill a slot.
