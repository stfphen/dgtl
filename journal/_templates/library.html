The trust economy

Audiences no longer reward reach for its own sake — they reward the creators they believe. The most valuable asset a creator holds isn't follower count; it's the credibility that makes a recommendation land.

Why matching beats size

The right 200,000 will out-convert the wrong two million every time. Fit — of audience, of voice, of category — is the variable that decides whether content reads as authentic or as an ad.

Inside the network

A curated roster is selected for exactly that fit, then matched to a brand's goals and measured on what it moved.

What comes next

The publications that win will be the ones platforming creators as journalists platform sources — with context, craft and receipts.
