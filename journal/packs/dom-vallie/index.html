Dom Vallie went from a Kitchener bedroom to a two-time JUNO nominee.

The Toronto-born, Kitchener-raised rapper writes, records and directs from the inside out. Inside the self-made sound that earned “Been Himma” a JUNO nod — and the DGTL Season 1 studio it grew up around.

Dom Vallie makes rap that sounds like it was built alone at 3 a.m. and meant to be heard by everyone — and the JUNO Awards agreed, handing the Toronto-born, Kitchener-raised artist a 2023 Rap Single of the Year nomination for “Been Himma.”

Vallie writes, records and directs from the inside out. “Nights,” the 2017 mixtape that opened his catalogue, was the sound of a teenager teaching himself the room. By “Been Himma” he’d turned that self-made instinct into more than five million Spotify streams and a national award nod — and in 2025 the Recording Academy of Canada came back, nominating his project “See You When I See You” for Rap Album/EP of the Year.

A self-made sound, built one late night at a time

The through-line in Vallie’s catalogue isn’t a trend — it’s authorship. He treats melody, cadence and vision as one job, moving between experimental and melodic hip-hop without borrowing anyone else’s blueprint. That’s the rarer thing to platform: not a viral moment, but an artist with a point of view that holds up across a mixtape, a breakout single and an award-nominated EP.

Two JUNO nominations in three years — and not one of them chased a trend.

— The DGTL Influence view

From a Toronto studio floor to a national stage

Vallie is one of the artists in DGTL Season 1 — the creatives who passed through the original DGTL Toronto studio at 487 Adelaide St. West. It’s a community of visual media makers, producers and touring artists, and Vallie is the music end of it: proof that a self-directed record made in a small room can end up on a JUNO shortlist. DGTL is featuring his work here, and pointing its audience toward the real thing — his catalogue, on the platforms below.

Why it matters
  • Two JUNO Award nominations — Rap Single of the Year (2023) and Rap Album/EP of the Year (2025).
  • “Been Himma” crossed five million Spotify streams and became his breakout.
  • Writes, records and directs his own work — authorship over algorithm.
  • Part of the DGTL Season 1 studio community in Toronto.

The arc — a self-taught debut in 2017, a JUNO-nominated single in 2023, a JUNO-nominated project in 2025 — reads like a plan, not a fluke. That’s the profile worth watching: an artist who keeps leveling up on his own terms.

FAQ

People also ask

Who is Dom Vallie?
Dom Vallie is a Toronto-born, Kitchener-raised rapper, singer and songwriter known for a self-produced, melodic-but-experimental take on hip-hop. He is a two-time JUNO Award nominee.
What did Dom Vallie get a JUNO nomination for?
His single “Been Himma” earned a 2023 JUNO nomination for Rap Single of the Year, and his project “See You When I See You” was nominated for Rap Album/EP of the Year in 2025.
Where can I listen to Dom Vallie?
On Spotify, Apple Music and YouTube — all linked from this profile — plus the official music videos featured in the gallery above.
Is Dom Vallie part of the DGTL community?
Yes — Dom Vallie is one of the artists in DGTL Season 1, the creative community around the original DGTL Toronto studio at 487 Adelaide St. West. DGTL is featuring his work; his releases are his own.
