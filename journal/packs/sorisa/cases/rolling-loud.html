Sorisa at Rolling Loud: a teenager on a giant's stage.

In May 2026, a 16-year-old from Toronto made his festival debut at Rolling Loud Orlando — on a 2026 lineup with Playboi Carti and Don Toliver, barely a year after his first SoundCloud uploads.

Rolling Loud is where the biggest names in rap set up their year. In May 2026, on the Orlando lineup — alongside headliners like Playboi Carti and Don Toliver — was a Toronto teenager who, eighteen months earlier, was uploading songs to SoundCloud from his bedroom. Sorisa's festival debut is the kind of jump that usually takes a career.

It's a scale most artists spend years climbing toward: the crowds, the production, the peers. For Sorisa to land there as a first festival — and this young — says the momentum behind "U Look So Good In Fall" and his Atlantic debut EP I Love Colours wasn't a single viral spike. It was a real audience, showing up in a real field, in Florida heat.

A first festival on a headliner's stage. That's not a booking — that's a signal.

— The DGTL Influence view

Why the booking matters

Rolling Loud doesn't fill its stages with unknowns. The debut came the same month Sorisa signed with The Feldman Agency, one of Canada's largest booking agencies — the live-business infrastructure catching up to the streaming numbers. Together they mark the moment a bedroom project became a touring act with a machine behind it.

The 2026 leap
  • Debut EP I Love Colours out on Atlantic Records (March 2026).
  • Signed to The Feldman Agency (May 2026).
  • Festival debut at Rolling Loud Orlando (May 2026).
  • Booked for First Class Fest at Sobeys Stadium, Toronto (August 2026).

What comes next

The road from Orlando runs straight back home: an August 2026 slot at First Class Fest, at the Bowl at Sobeys Stadium in Toronto — a hometown stage, a long way up from Lee's Palace. For a brand tracking where culture is heading, the Rolling Loud booking is the clearest possible tell: this isn't a moment, it's a trajectory. And DGTL has been documenting it since the very first set.
