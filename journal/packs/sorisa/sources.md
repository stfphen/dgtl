## Verified facts

- Canadian singer/songwriter from Toronto; electropop / EDM / hyperpop; viral single "U Look So Good In Fall"; signed to Atlantic Records, repped by The Feldman Agency — Wikipedia; Billboard Canada.
- Stage name from Spanish *sonrisa* ("smile") minus the "n"; "don't forget who you are"; Latino heritage; attends high school in Toronto — Wikipedia (citing Wishe Music).
- Started as **brokeboyhappy** on SoundCloud; rebranded to Sorisa **September 2025** — Wikipedia.
- **"U Look So Good In Fall"** released Aug 22, 2025 (Onze); went viral on TikTok — Wikipedia; Billboard Canada.
- **Concert debut as headliner at Lee's Palace, November 2025** — free show filled the 500-person venue within ten minutes — Wikipedia (citing Billboard Canada / Feldman). Concert Archives lists the date as Nov 13, 2025.
- Debut EP **I Love Colours** (5 tracks) released March 10, 2026 under Atlantic Records; earlier EP **98/99** (Oct 17, 2025, Onze) — Wikipedia; Apple Music.
- **I Love Colours Experience** mini-tour: Mod Club (Toronto), Mercury Theatre (NYC) — Wikipedia.
- **Signed The Feldman Agency, May 2026**; **festival debut at Rolling Loud Orlando, May 2026** (2026 lineup incl. Playboi Carti, Don Toliver as headliners) — Wikipedia; Billboard Canada; CelebrityAccess.
- **Billboard Canada 2026 Artist to Watch**; described as "equal parts addicting, colourful and electrifying," compared to Nate Sib and 2hollis — Billboard Canada.
- Booked for **First Class Fest at the Bowl, Sobeys Stadium, Toronto, August 2026** — Wikipedia (citing Feldman).
- Latest single "Melody I Can Finish" — May 19, 2026 — Wikipedia; antiMusic.
- 20+ tracks released since 2024 (count from discography table) — Wikipedia.
