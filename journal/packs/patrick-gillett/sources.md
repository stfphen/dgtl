## Verified vs. user-asserted (fact discipline)

- **Verified** (Wikipedia + press): Gillett co-founded Down with Webster in 1998 and plays guitar/vocals; band origin (Toronto, the Beaches); Universal Motown signing (2009); hits "Whoa Is Me," "Your Man" (2× platinum, Music Canada), "Rich Girl$," "Chills," "She's Dope"; "Party for Your Life" #2 Canadian albums; 6 JUNO nominations (band); 2024 reunion; 2025 singles "Okae" and "All Night"; Honors project; Fake Shark 2025 collaboration.
- **User-asserted** (DGTL Season 1 brief): that Patrick Gillett was part of the DGTL Toronto studio community at 487 Adelaide St. West. Stated as provided; DGTL is framed as *featuring* him.
- **Attributed, not bio-confirmed:** his personal **@patgillett** on Instagram and X. Widely attributed across sources but the profiles sit behind a login wall, so they weren't read directly. In `sameAs` — confirm with Patrick before publishing, or remove if wrong.
- **Deliberately omitted:** no standalone official band website was confirmed (booking runs through agency pages); personal follower counts not verified.
