The Burns Twins built an act out of looking identical.

Toronto brothers Will and Joe Burns turn an accident of genetics into a format: two identical faces, one running gag, and a stranger who can’t tell which twin they just met.

The Burns Twins — Toronto brothers Will and Joe Burns — turned an accident of genetics into a format: identical faces, one running gag, and a stranger who can’t work out which twin they just talked to.

It is a deceptively hard thing to make look easy. The Burns Twins build “wholesome twin pranks” — déjà-vu bits, time-traveller gags, quadruplet mix-ups — that only land because both brothers are on screen, in sync, selling the same beat at the same time. The concept is the special effect, and the twins are the effect.

The gimmick that only two people on earth can do

Plenty of creators chase a trend; almost none have a format nobody can copy. Being identical twins is a moat. Every video is a small, clean piece of misdirection — set the rule, break it, watch the reaction — and it reads instantly in the first second of a scroll, which is exactly what short-form rewards. That repeatability is why the act travels from a Toronto street to a US campus without losing the joke.

The concept is the special effect — and the twins are the effect.

— The DGTL Influence view

One brand, two brothers, three platforms

Will and Joe run the Burns Twins as a single act, not two solo channels — the same @theburnstwins identity across TikTok, Instagram and YouTube, because the story is the pair, not either brother alone. They are part of DGTL Season 1, the creative community around the original DGTL Toronto studio at 487 Adelaide St. West. DGTL is featuring the duo here and pointing to their real channels, linked below.

Why it works
  • A format no one can copy — being identical twins is the whole mechanic.
  • Hooks in the first second: set a rule, break it, catch the reaction.
  • One shared brand across TikTok, Instagram and YouTube — easy for fans to follow.
  • Toronto-based, part of the DGTL Season 1 community.

Wholesome, repeatable, and built on the one thing a competitor literally cannot replicate — that is a rare foundation for a creator brand, and it is why the Burns Twins are worth watching as the act scales.

1 concept
One idea, endlessly remixable. Twin-confusion — déjà vu, time travel, quadruplets — gives the Burns Twins a format they can run anywhere without wearing it out.

Who are the Burns Twins?
The Burns Twins are Will and Joe Burns, identical twin brothers from Toronto, Ontario. They are content creators known for wholesome twin-confusion pranks across TikTok, Instagram and YouTube.

Original prank and comedy videos built around their identical looks — déjà-vu, time-traveller and quadruplet bits — where strangers can’t tell the two brothers apart.
