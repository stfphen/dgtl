Shane Boyer films New York hands-free for Ray-Ban Meta.

Ray-Ban Meta's smart glasses record the world from exactly where your eyes are — which is the whole point of a Shane Boyer video. The @shaneboyerr x Ray-Ban Meta partnership is the rare brand fit where the product IS the format.

Most creators have to bolt a brand onto their content. For Shane Boyer (@shaneboyerr) and Ray-Ban Meta, the brand IS the content — smart glasses that record from exactly where his eyes are, which is the entire premise of a Shane Boyer POV video. It's the cleanest kind of brand fit: the product doesn't interrupt the format, it becomes it.

Boyer built an audience of 2.5M+ on POV New York — the ledge, the subway platform, the golden-hour rooftop. Ray-Ban Meta hands him a camera he doesn't have to hold, so the frame gets even more first-person. When he says he's letting the glasses plan his day in the city, the ad reads as a genuinely useful demo, because it is one.

When the product is the format

In his "letting my Ray-Ban Meta glasses plan my day in NYC" film, the AI features aren't a spec list — they're the plot. A second post, exploring NYC with the Ray-Ban Meta smart glasses, does what Boyer does best: point the frame at the city and let the mood carry it, this time with the camera literally on his face. Both are tagged #RayBanMetaPartner, and both feel like his own work.

The glasses don't interrupt his format. They complete it.

— The DGTL Influence view on Shane Boyer x Ray-Ban Meta

Why this partnership works

Because Boyer already owns first-person New York. Ray-Ban Meta didn't have to teach his audience a new behaviour — POV is what they came for. So a hands-free camera reads as an upgrade to the thing they love, not an ad break inside it. That's the difference between renting attention and earning it, and it's exactly what a creator-led brief is supposed to buy.

Why the Ray-Ban Meta fit works
  • The product is the format — hands-free POV is literally what Boyer makes.
  • The AI demo doubles as story: useful and watchable at once.
  • An audience that already came for first-person New York.
  • #RayBanMetaPartner content that reads as his own, not an interruption.

For a tech brand trying to make new hardware feel native, that's the whole game: find the creator whose format the product completes, and let them shoot. Ray-Ban Meta found one in Shane Boyer.

FAQ

People also ask

What did Shane Boyer do for Ray-Ban Meta?
Shane Boyer (@shaneboyerr) created POV films for Ray-Ban Meta smart glasses as a #RayBanMetaPartner, shooting New York hands-free. One film shows him letting the glasses' AI plan his day in NYC; another explores the city through the smart glasses.
Why are Ray-Ban Meta glasses a good fit for Shane Boyer?
Because Boyer's signature is first-person POV video, and Ray-Ban Meta records from exactly where his eyes are. The product becomes the format rather than interrupting it, so the partnership content reads as his own work.
Where can I watch Shane Boyer's Ray-Ban Meta videos?
On his TikTok, @shaneboyerr — both #RayBanMetaPartner films are posted there and linked from this profile. He also posts on Instagram as @shaneboyer_.
Who is Shane Boyer?
Shane Boyer is an American content creator and POV filmmaker known for cinematic New York City and skateboarding videos, with 2.5M+ TikTok followers and 235K+ on Instagram. His brand partners have included Ray-Ban Meta, Polaroid, YSL and Canada Goose.
