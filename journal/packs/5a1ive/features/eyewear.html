5a1ive wires luxury frames for the night.

Beyond the watches, 5a1ive rebuilds Ray-Ban, Oakley, Diesel and Burberry eyewear with aluminum wire and crystal — the 55555stuff shades line, made, in the brand's words, for the nightlife scene.

The 5a1ive signature doesn't stop at the wrist. Under 55555stuff, the Toronto designer takes luxury eyewear — Ray-Ban, Oakley, Diesel, Burberry — and rebuilds each pair with a lattice of aluminum wire and crystal, a look the brand pitches squarely at the nightlife scene.

It's the same instinct as the watches: take a recognisable object and make it stranger and more expensive-looking. A blue Oakley shield gets caged in fine silver wire; a pair of Ray-Bans is criss-crossed until the frame reads as sculpture; Diesel and Burberry frames get the same industrial-couture treatment. The result is eyewear that looks engineered rather than bought.

Industrial couture, one pair at a time

Each style in the line carries a code and a base house — CS2 on Ray-Ban, BW1 and BW2 on Oakley, BW3 and CS1 on Diesel, CS4 on Burberry, CS3 on an Oakley shield. Prices run from about $525 to $615 USD. Like the watches, these are hand-built pieces, not a mass run — the wire is set by hand, so no two pairs sit exactly alike.

Recognisable frames, rebuilt until they look engineered, not bought.

— The DGTL Influence view on 5a1ive

Built for the scene it's photographed in

The 55555stuff aesthetic is explicit about where this lives — the store tags itself with cyberpunk, rocker and 2077 references, and the eyewear is designed for exactly that room: dark, loud, camera-heavy. It's the accessory version of the watch thesis, an object built to be seen at night. See the full range on the 55555stuff shades collection.

The eyewear at a glance
  • Luxury frames — Ray-Ban, Oakley, Diesel, Burberry — rebuilt, not branded over.
  • Hand-set aluminum wire and crystal on every pair.
  • Seven coded styles, roughly $525–$615 USD.
  • Designed for the nightlife scene the brand is built around.

For a fashion or nightlife brand, the eyewear line is proof the 5a1ive look extends past a single product — a consistent hand across categories is exactly what makes a designer worth building a campaign around.

FAQ

People also ask

What is the 5a1ive eyewear line?
It's the 55555stuff Shades collection — luxury sunglasses by 5a1ive that rebuild Ray-Ban, Oakley, Diesel and Burberry frames with aluminum wire and crystal, designed for the nightlife scene.
How much are 5a1ive sunglasses?
The 55555stuff shades run from about $525 to $615 USD per pair, depending on the base frame and style. Each is hand-built.
Which frames does 5a1ive customise?
The line is built on Ray-Ban, Oakley, Diesel and Burberry frames, sold under codes like CS1–CS4 and BW1–BW3 on 55555stuff.com.
Where can I buy 5a1ive eyewear?
On 55555stuff.com under the Shades collection, or by reaching 5a1ive through Instagram (@5a1ive / @55555stuff).
