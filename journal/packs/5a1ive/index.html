5a1ive turns G-Shocks into one-of-one art.

The Toronto designer behind 55555stuff hand-sets crystals onto G-Shocks and luxury frames — one-of-one pieces that have reached Yung Fazo, the Toronto Raptors, and Drake's Iceman launch, a 2026 campaign he worked on with DGTL.

On Drake's 2026 launch

@5a1ive is a verified account with 16.2K followers and 161 posts as of July 17, 2026. Iceman-campaign involvement per DGTL, which co-managed the project; the album's Toronto rollout is widely reported. Product details from 55555stuff.com.

5a1ive doesn't decorate watches — he rebuilds them. Working out of Toronto under the label 55555stuff, the designer buries G-Shock cases under hand-set crystals and raw mineral clusters, turning a stock Casio into a one-of-one object that reads more like jewellery than a watch.

The signature is excess with control. An amethyst geode erupts off the bezel of a DW-6900; a GA-2100 CasiOak disappears under a field of tightly-packed stones; a red build made for the Toronto Raptors glitters like a championship ring. Every piece starts from a customer's colour and a loose brief, then 5a1ive takes full creative control — no two builds are the same, and that's the entire pitch.

A craft nobody else is doing at this level

Most custom G-Shock sellers swap a bezel or add a bracelet. 5a1ive treats the watch as raw canvas — cutting, setting and sculpting until the Casio underneath is barely visible. The same hand shows up on the 55555stuff eyewear line, where Ray-Ban, Oakley, Diesel and Burberry frames are wrapped in aluminum wire and crystal for what the brand calls the nightlife scene. It reads as one coherent language: industrial hardware, softened and iced until it looks expensive and a little dangerous.

A stock Casio goes in. A one-of-one comes out.

— The DGTL Influence view on 5a1ive

From a Shopify store to the rap scene's wrists

The work travels because the right people wear it. 5a1ive pieces have shown up on Yung Fazo, Osamason, Xavier Trudeau, Slayr and Rio, and the brand's gallery documents a red-crystal build worn by the Toronto Raptors. In 2026 that reach hit a new ceiling: as part of Drake's Iceman launch — a Toronto-wide rollout 5a1ive worked on with DGTL — the studio produced custom G-Shocks tied to Drake's world, including pieces for Drake and his son Adonis (per DGTL). The verified @5a1ive account turns each build into a viral clip, from "testing Drake fans" to flying a piece to Bloody Osiris in Paris. The full catalogue lives at 55555stuff.com.

Why 5a1ive stands out
  • Every G-Shock is one-of-one — customer colour in, full creative control out.
  • Raw crystal clusters and dense stone-setting, not off-the-shelf bezels.
  • A matching eyewear line: Ray-Ban, Oakley, Diesel and Burberry, wire-wrapped and iced.
  • Reach from the rap scene to the Raptors to Drake's Iceman launch.

For a brand chasing the rage-and-nightlife audience, 5a1ive is the rare partner whose product is already the marketing — a physical object people photograph, wear and want. That's the profile DGTL Influence looks for: a maker with a look nobody can copy and a scene that carries it for free.

FAQ

People also ask

Who is 5a1ive?
5a1ive is a Toronto-based designer and the maker behind 55555stuff, known for one-of-one custom G-Shock watches encrusted with hand-set crystals and raw mineral clusters, plus a line of wire-wrapped luxury eyewear.
What is 55555stuff?
55555stuff (55555stuff.com) is 5a1ive's label, selling custom crystal G-Shocks from about $500 USD and augmented Ray-Ban, Oakley, Diesel and Burberry eyewear. Every watch is a one-of-one commission.
How much is a custom 5a1ive G-Shock?
Custom G-Shocks start around $500 USD and run to roughly $600 for models like the Bey Blade build. Each is handmade to order with a 2–3 week lead time.
Who has worn 5a1ive?
5a1ive pieces have appeared on Yung Fazo, Osamason, Xavier Trudeau, Slayr and Rio, and on the Toronto Raptors. In 2026, 5a1ive also produced custom G-Shocks for Drake's Iceman launch — including pieces for Drake and his son Adonis — on a project co-managed with DGTL.
