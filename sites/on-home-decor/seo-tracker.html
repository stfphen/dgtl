How to run the review push

The rules that make reviews actually move rankings in 2026.

Pace it — don't dump. 2–3 new reviews per week beats 30 at once then silence. Reviews from the last 30 days carry full weight; older ones decay.

Seed keywords. Ask clients to name the service + neighbourhood in their own words ("paint colour consultation for our Rosedale home"). Google ties that phrase to your profile.

Reply to every one (aim 80%+), working the service name in naturally. Don't fear a 4.5–4.9 average — a flat 5.0 can read as fake.
