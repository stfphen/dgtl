Questions

The short answers.

What does it cost?

Nothing. The Journal is our editorial property; featuring great creators is the point. Paid campaign work is separate and always negotiated with you directly.

What rights am I giving DGTL?

A non-exclusive, revocable licence to feature the material you submit on DGTL-owned properties — you keep ownership, and you can revoke with 30 days notice. Edits, broadcast use and AI-training exclusion are separate opt-ins. Read the full terms.

Can I change my mind later?

Yes. The licence is revocable with 30 days written notice, and every marketing consent has an unsubscribe.

Will my content be used to train AI?

Not unless you say so — the AI-training opt-out is on by default in the application.

How long does review take?

About 10 business days. Every application is read by a human on the editorial team.
