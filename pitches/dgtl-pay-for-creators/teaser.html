of your money is ever held by DGTL

We are the referee, not the bank.

The fee goes from the brand's account into escrow, and out of escrow into yours. It never passes through DGTL. We write the invoice, check the delivery, and break the tie if there is a disagreement — we never hold the balance.

The honest flip side: because it is your account, keeping access to it is on you. We set it up with you and it recovers by email, but nobody at DGTL can move your funds — and that cuts both ways.
