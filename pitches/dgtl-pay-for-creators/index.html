What actually changes

The work takes a day. Getting paid takes everything else.

You shot it, cut it, posted it, and then the part nobody talks about starts: the invoice, the silence, the follow-up, the "it's with finance," and the transfer that finally arrives smaller than the number you agreed to.

None of that is about how good the work was. It is about the fact that nothing connected what you agreed to, what you delivered, and what got paid — so every step needed a human to chase it.

DGTL Pay connects those three things, and puts the money somewhere you can see before you pick up a camera.

How it usually goes
  1. You agree a fee. You have no way to know the money exists.
  2. You deliver, then send an invoice into a system you cannot see.
  3. You wait. You follow up. You follow up again.
  4. It lands, minus fees that were decided by where you live.
How it goes on the rail
  1. You agree a fee, and the brand funds it into escrow.
  2. You can see it is funded before you start shooting.
  3. You deliver; delivery is checked against what you agreed.
  4. It is released to your account. Same fee, wherever you live.

Non-custodial · by design

We are the referee, not the bank.

The fee goes from the brand's account into escrow, and out of escrow into yours. It never passes through DGTL. We write the invoice, check the delivery, and act as a tie-breaker if there is a disagreement — we never hold the balance.

That means there is no version of this where DGTL sits on your money, delays your money, or loses your money, because we never have it in the first place.

The honest flip side: because it is your account, you are responsible for keeping access to it. We set the account up with you during onboarding and it recovers by email — no seed phrase written on paper — but nobody at DGTL can move your funds, and that cuts both ways.

One more honest thing. DGTL Pay is in build and has not paid a creator yet, so there is no average payout time or creator count on this page — there is nothing real to put there. The brand logos above are DGTL Group's agency clients, not DGTL Pay users. When Phase 1 has produced real numbers, we will publish those instead.

Straight answers

What creators actually ask.

Do I need to know anything about crypto?

No. You need an account to be paid into — what the industry calls a wallet. We set it up with you during onboarding, it takes a few minutes, and it recovers by email rather than by a phrase you have to write down.

USDC is a digital dollar: one USDC is one US dollar. You cash out to your bank through a licensed partner in your country.

Does it cost me anything?

DGTL does not take a cut of your fee. There is no subscription, no minimum and no deposit.

Cashing out to your local bank goes through a licensed partner and their rates apply — that part is theirs, not ours, and you will see it before you use it.

What if the brand says I did not deliver?

Release needs two signatures out of three: yours, the brand's, and DGTL as tie-breaker. The brand cannot simply take the money back, and DGTL cannot release it to either side alone.

If there is a disagreement, it is judged against the terms you both agreed before the money was funded — a written document, not a memory.

Do I have to buy $DGTL?

No — and you could not if you wanted to. It has not been minted, issued, offered or sold, and there is no way for anyone to acquire it today.

If it ever activates, it would be earned by working on the platform, never bought. It will never be required to get paid.

How fast does it actually land?

We are not going to give you a number, because DGTL Pay has not paid anyone yet and any figure we printed here would be invented.

What we can tell you is what removes the delay: the money is already funded before you start, and release is triggered by delivery being checked rather than by a monthly payment run.

Is my country supported?

Not all of them yet. Phase 1 opens one corridor at a time, starting where DGTL already pays the most creators, and a corridor only opens when a licensed partner covers it properly.
