01 — The opportunity

The trust is already out there. It just isn't working for you yet.

Your audience spends hours a day with creators they trust — and about three seconds with ads they don't. Every launch that leads with a cold ad is paying full price to introduce itself to people who were already listening to someone else.

Influencer activation flips that: the introduction comes from a voice your audience already believes, and the content it produces keeps selling long after the post goes live. That's the upside sitting untapped right now — and it's exactly the part we're built to close.

02 — Who you'd be working with

One team, end-to-end.

DGTL is a full-service creative and marketing group — branding, content, advertising and web, crafted with brilliance, attention, precision.

Everything below is built and run in-house. No handoffs, no middle layer — the people who match your creators are the people who brief them, license the content, cut it into ad creative, and optimize the spend behind it.

Pillar 01 · The activation

The right voices, saying it like they mean it.

We don't send a press kit to a spreadsheet of handles. We build a cast. Every creator is chosen for genuine fit with your product and the audience you're trying to move — whether that's one perfect voice or a synchronized roster.

  • i

    Creator matching, hand-picked

    Drawn from DGTL's network of creators and our public-figure PR list — filtered for niche fit, audience quality and real engagement, not follower counts.

  • ii

    Coordinated rollout

    Posts sequenced around your release or campaign window, so the launch lands as one wave — not scattered mentions weeks apart.

  • iii

    UGC + professional placements

    From native, shot-on-phone UGC to polished creator features — each brief matched to the format that converts in that creator's feed.

  • iv

    Organic reach, engineered

    Hooks, angles and messaging directed by us so every post earns exposure, shifts perception and pulls new audience toward the brand.

Works at any brand size — a single hand-picked creator for a focused niche play, or a full roster activation for a major release. Same system, scaled to the moment.

Pillar 02 · The amplification

Then we put money behind what's already winning.

Organic tells us which creators and angles hit. Paid makes them go further. This is where the activation stops being a moment and becomes a measurable conversion engine.

  • i

    Whitelisting & spark ads

    Winning posts run as paid ads from the creators' own handles — borrowed trust at ad-platform scale, far beyond their followers.

  • ii

    Conversion tracking & data

    Full pixel and server-side tracking, so every campaign is read against real revenue and tuned — nothing goes unmeasured.

  • iii

    Creative testing at speed

    Hooks, cuts and creators tested head-to-head; budget flows to the combinations that convert, automatically and continuously.

  • iv

    An ad engine, not an ad buy

    The best creator content becomes a durable paid system across Meta and TikTok — refreshed with new UGC before fatigue sets in.

Why this order? Because across the industry, great creative is the single biggest driver of ad results — more than targeting, more than budget. The activation manufactures the creative; the paid layer makes it compound.
