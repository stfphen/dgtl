Campaign money crosses borders on rails built for neither creators nor campaigns. So we took ourselves off the money path.

Nothing links what was agreed, what was delivered and what was paid — so month-end becomes a reconstruction across three systems. On DGTL Pay the invoice, the ledger line and the tax record all come out of the same event that released the funds.
