| 2026-07-25 | **`apps/` and `sites/` become top-level surfaces alongside `platform/` and the publishing tree — the monorepo has four surfaces, not two.** `apps/dgtl-os/` for standalone products; `sites/<client>/` for client deliverables that aren't tenant funnels. | DGTL OS was decided on 07-18 as a standalone product — *not a tenant, not an admin feature* — so filing it under `platform/` would contradict that and blur the boundary `CLAUDE.md` is built around. | 2026-07-25 | **Deploy artifacts, staging copies and zips are never committed beside their source.** Only the newest PolishStone source is tracked; the three `netlify-staging` copies and both zips (`zilHu3WT`, `polishstone-netlify-staging.zip`) are excluded. | Four near-identical 7.9 MB copies with no marker of which was live is the ambiguity that made this work un-committable in the first place — and ~31 MB of it was redundant. | 2026-07-25 | **Pitches follow the journal pack model — `pitches/<slug>/` with a `pitch.json` manifest — and the generic offer pages live in `pitches/_templates/`, not as pitches.** A pitch's assets stay in its own folder; pitch pages stay self-contained and are never refactored onto `journal/_shared/`. | One convention across both publishing surfaces means the deck index and deploy listing can be generated from manifests instead of hand-maintained, and adding a pitch is one folder from a template.
